## Other changes
- Results changed slightly because the models are now tuned. Tuned Logistic Regression scores nested-CV F1 = 0.909 ± 0.026 and ROC-AUC = 0.942 ± 0.032, against the baseline's F1 = 0.815. Gaussian Naive Bayes is best on the single hold-out split (F1 = 0.921) but not in nested CV. The paper reports both results and explains the difference.
- A new metric, at-risk recall (recall on Not Placed), is reported because it is what a placement cell needs. Logistic Regression finds 72 % of at-risk students.
- The subgroup audit found that at-risk recall is lowest for students with work experience (0.40) and for Mkt&Fin students (0.56). The paper therefore restricts use to adding students to a mentoring list.
- Not done: validation on a second institution's data, because no such dataset is available. It is listed as a limitation and as future work.
